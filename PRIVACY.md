# Privacy Statement — Exciting AI

**Exciting AI** is built on the principle of **local-first privacy and user data sovereignty**.

---

## Core Privacy Guarantees

### 1. 100% Local Storage by Default
- Your conversation sessions, prompt inputs, model parameters, and workspace files are stored directly on your personal computer in `Storage/chats/`.
- No user prompts or chat histories are ever sent to IDeV or third-party telemetry collectors.

### 2. Transparent & User-Controlled Web Access
- Exciting AI does not perform silent background web crawling.
- Real-time web research is triggered **only** when you explicitly activate the globe-shaped **Web Access** button in the chat composer.
- When disabled, inference runs strictly within the selected model's context window with zero external network search calls.

### 3. Zero Trackers, Telemetry, or Analytics
- Exciting AI contains **no hidden trackers**, analytics SDKs, Google Analytics, telemetry beacons, or advertising profiles.
- Your session history remains your property, completely under your local control.

### 4. Independent Model Freedom (No Vendor Lock-in)
- Access to 1,927 frontier models from top AI labs without requiring vendor accounts or proprietary lock-in.
- Bring your own API keys or use the included free tier out of the box.
